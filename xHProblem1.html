Hardware Problem

Computer Won't Turn On

The computer does not start when the power button is pressed.

Possible Causes

  • Loose or disconnected power cable.
  • Faulty electrical outlet.
  • Power supply switch is turned off.
  • Defective power supply unit (PSU).
  • Damaged power cable.

Possible Solutions

1. Check the Power Cable

Make sure the power cable is properly connected to the computer and electrical outlet.

2. Check the Electrical Outlet

Test the outlet using a suitable working device.

3. Check the Power Supply Switch

If your computer has a switch at the back of its power supply, make sure it is turned on.

4. Seek Technical Assistance

If the computer still does not turn on, ask a qualified technician to inspect it. Never open the power supply unit yourself.

How to Prevent it

  • Check power cables regularly.
  • Use a reliable electrical outlet.
  • Avoid damaging or bending power cables.
  • Keep the computer properly maintained.
